M2-WReopens Y4

Adopt the passing combination, including each row's own-year weather?

Why it matters

Y4 kept weather as one factor per line for the target year. The passing combination prices each row with its own address's weather for its year, against that address's normal. Responses are fitted on emerging data: storm on wind, subsidence on dry summers, escape of water on freezing days, fire on the wildfire index, flood on rainfall, and glass and hail on hail days. For 2025 rows it reads the 2025 weather file, which has every test address. Without the row weather, the rest of the combination does not clear the test (cross-validation interval −7,014 to 72,769).

What the data shows

On its own, row weather adds little (cross-validation +4,289 [−2,975, 11,706]), and with Part 1's responses it adds nothing. It helps alongside the other changes, chiefly in buildings (+14,730 of the 37,526). In Part 1, own-year weather in the trees did not help (the Y4 evidence). The difference here is that emerging weather is much stronger (hail days doubled, wildfire index tripled) and the responses are fitted per peril.

(a) Adopt K9 plus row-level weatherRecommended

Hazards for buildings fire and flood, the Part 1 tree in contents, gender in motor, and each row's own-year weather. Y4 changes from one factor per line to a factor per row for this market. The write-up shows the weather responses and the 2025 factors.

(b) K9 with Y4 as decided

Keeps Y4. Does not clear P2-V, so strictly the check file's shape would stay.

(c) Keep the check file's shape

Nothing has passed except (a).

Recommendation: (a).It is the only candidate that clears the test you set. The weather term uses information every 2025 row carries, without borrowing from the future: the 2025 weather is a given input, like the address.
M2-NTwo-way door; moves the 2025 level by line

How much should the new-business relativity move for a 76% new-business book?

Why it matters

The level chain sets each line's level on the training mix, which is 86% renewals. The test book is 76% new business. If the model's new-business relativity is off, every line's 2025 level is off by most of that error. The 2024 tests cannot show it, because 2024 is mostly renewals. That is why the new-business layer failed the shape test above while still mattering for the level.

M2-BAmends P2-P

May the public board be used once, as a check on the new-business level?

Why it matters

Under P2-P the final file is chosen on our own validation, never on the public board. On the new-business level our validation is blind: our data has few new-business rows. The public board scores about 70,000 test rows, about 53,000 of them new business. It is the only evidence there is on how the 2025 new-business book claims.

(a) One paired check, disclosedRecommended

Submit the improved file twice: with M2-N (a) and with your M2-N choice. The two differ only in the new-business factor. Keep your choice unless the public score falls. One comparison, decided before it runs and written up as such: a check of one assumption, not tuning to the board.

(b) No board use, as P2-P stands

Go with the M2-N choice untested.

Recommendation: (a).It is the only test of the largest assumption in the 2025 level. Both files share every other choice, so the paired difference is mostly the new-business level, and the large-claim noise largely cancels.
