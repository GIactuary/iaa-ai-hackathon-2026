Which model goes into the final file?

Why it matters

Kaggle takes one selected file per part, scored on the private two-thirds of the 2025 rows. The hold-out was our last untouched evidence. Every choice below now rests on evidence we have looked at, so the write-up states that the choice was made after the hold-out was opened, and why.

What changed in buildings

The frozen buildings shape prices fire and flood as frequency times severity with address hazards (wildfire normal, distance to river, elevation), and gives each row its own-year weather. It never passed alone. It entered as part of a combination that passed on pooled cross-validation, where buildings supplied 14,730 of the 37,526 saved. Out of time it had already lost (−12,065). The hold-out now loses in both readings. The design flagged the risk before the first run: the emerging wildfire index sits at about 0.5, against 0.15 at most in Part 1, so the fire term extrapolates.
