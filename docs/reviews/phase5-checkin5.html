How every test on this page reads. Each mix is scored on a year its weights never saw: weights fitted on 2023 predict 2024, and weights fitted on 2024 predict 2023. "Saved" is the drop in total Tweedie deviance against the W2 GLM, with every line held at its actual total, so only ranking counts. The keep rule needs a saving in both years and a 95% customer-bootstrap interval on 2023 above zero. Example: in buildings the mix saves 42,006 on 2023, interval 18,728 to 62,383, and 52,783 on 2024, so it passes.

Step 8 · family weights (J-model)

What the mix means for a price

Each line's 2025 price would be a weighted average of the family models that earned weight. The weights are fitted on past years' predictions and must sum to one. In pricing terms:

  • Motor: about two-thirds the EBM by peril (one curve per factor, per peril, for frequency and for severity) and one-third the GLM times its small boosted correction.
  • Buildings: about four-fifths the GLM times its boosted correction (led by elevation, voluntary excess, flood zone and history) and one-fifth CatBoost.
  • Contents: nine-tenths the standardised spline GLM if the mix is used there; otherwise the W2 GLM as now.

How to read this. Take a motor policy where the EBM by peril says 600 and the corrected GLM says 450, with weights 0.664 and 0.336. The arithmetic mix charges 0.664 × 600 + 0.336 × 450 = 549.5. The geometric mix charges 6000.664 × 4500.336 = 544.6: it averages relativities the way a rating plan multiplies them. The W2 GLM itself takes no weight, because the boosted model already is the GLM times a correction.

After the verdicts

What freezing the model involves

  • Fit each chosen family model on every non-hold-out row of 2021 to 2024 and predict the 2025 rows.
  • Mix them with the chosen weights, then apply the decided level: the line excess factor, the off-balance to the weather-normalised indication, and the 2025 weather as one factor per line.
  • Price blank contents fields at the average of the known levels (P8-R2) in every model, check the lowest and highest prices, and write the second submission file for you to upload.
  • Then phase 6: the referral rule and model-disagreement threshold, written before they run.
