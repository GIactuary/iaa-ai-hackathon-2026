How does the copy of the host's pages leave the repository?

Why it matters

The data and the host's pages may not be redistributed, and a file deleted in a new commit stays readable in every earlier one. Once the repository is public, anyone can clone that history; deleting it afterwards does not recall the copies.

(a) Rewrite both branches to drop the file from every commitRecommended

I install git-filter-repo, keep a full backup of the repository as it is now, rewrite main and intake-and-plan, and rerun the scan to show the file is gone from every commit. Commit order, dates and messages are kept, so the record that each design was committed before its test still stands. Every commit hash changes: the hashes quoted in evidence files and the AI log will point to the old commits, so I commit the old-to-new map beside the scan. About 30 minutes.

(b) Publish a new repository with one commit of the current files

Simplest and certain, but it gives up the version history the panel grades and the proof that rules came before results.

Recommendation: (a).It removes the copy completely and keeps the history, which is the best evidence we have on guardrails.

What happens to the 2025 reference folder?

Why it matters

reference/2025/ holds our own 2025 preparation (the decided model, the notebook kit, the results on identical folds) and reruns of three other teams' public notebooks. Kaggle publishes notebooks under Apache 2.0, which cannot be relicensed under our MIT licence, and the reruns carry those notebooks' row-level fixes to the 2025 data, whose licence we have not checked. The 2026 entry does not use any of it; CLAUDE.md and docs/LESSONS_2025.md describe the 2025 lessons in our own words.

(a) Remove the other teams' reruns and the leaderboard copy; keep our own 2025 workRecommended

In the same rewrite as PUB-H, drop reference/2025/winner_reproduction/, reference/2025/other_winners/ and reference/2025/results/leaderboard.json from every commit. Our 2025 code and results stay, and the README notes what was removed and why.

(b) Remove the whole reference/2025/ folder

Leaves nothing from 2025 to explain. The playbook and lessons still say what 2025 taught; the code behind them goes.

(c) Keep everything and add Apache 2.0 notices for the reruns

Keeps the record whole, but the row-level 2025 data fixes would still be published, and the licence mix needs care that the deadline does not leave time for.

Recommendation: (a).It removes exactly what is not ours to license and keeps the preparation that shaped how this entry was built.
