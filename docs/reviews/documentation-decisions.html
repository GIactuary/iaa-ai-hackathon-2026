Where does premium tax sit in the office premium?

Why it matters

The report must show, for each of the 20 reference policies, how our risk premium becomes an office premium. The expense table gives: fixed expense 85 CU per policy; variable expense 15%, commission (broker 20%, direct 0%), premium tax 8% and target profit 8%, each as a share of premium; catastrophe reinsurance 12% of property risk premium. I read "property" as both buildings and contents. The only open point is the tax.

(a) Tax in the permissible loss ratioRecommended

Office premium = (risk premium + cat load + 85) ÷ (1 − 15% − commission − 8% tax − 8% profit). For a broker policy the divisor is 0.49.

(b) Tax on top

Office premium = (risk premium + cat load + 85) ÷ (1 − 15% − commission − 8%), then × 1.08. About 7% lower for broker policies: the first motor reference policy reads 2,644 instead of 2,848.

Recommendation: (a).The table states the tax as a share of premium, as it does commission and profit, so all three are treated alike. This also matches Canadian rate-filing practice, where premium tax is a variable expense inside the permissible loss ratio.

On what basis is the build-up shown?

Why it matters

Four reference policies are short rows: one starts on 22 November (0.11 of a year in 2025), one is cancelled after 25 days. Our prediction is the expected cost for the row's period. The written premium in the data is annual: the 0.11-year row shows 180.72 CU, against 230.80 for a full-year buildings policy. On a period basis, a 25-day contents row would carry the full 85 CU fixed expense.

(a) AnnualRecommended

Risk premium annualised (prediction ÷ exposure), office premium for a year, set beside the annual written premium.

(b) The row's period, full fixed expense

The 25-day contents row: risk premium 4.8 CU, office premium 184 CU, mostly the fixed expense.

(c) The row's period, fixed expense pro rata

Consistent, but cannot be compared with the annual written premium without annualising again.

Recommendation: (a).A price is quoted for a year, the fixed expense is per policy per year, and the comparison with the current premium only works like for like.

How is the code repository published?

Why it matters

The panel grades "a well-structured code repository with version control", and AI tools screen it first. A winner must license the code under an OSI-approved licence. The data may not be redistributed, so it stays out. The repository currently has no remote. Before anything is published I scan the whole history, not just the current files, for data, copies of the host's pages, credentials and anything from outside this entry.

(a) Public GitHub repository, full history, MIT licenceRecommended

You create it under your account and push it; I prepare the README, licence and scan. The history shows how the work was built: the designs committed before each test run, which is our strongest evidence on guardrails.

(b) Private repository, access for the organisers

Not permanent or public. Depends on the form accepting a private link, which it may not.

(c) A zipped snapshot without history

Loses the version control the criterion names.

Recommendation: (a).Version control is graded, and the commit history is the proof that rules came before results. The scan protects the data rule and your name.
