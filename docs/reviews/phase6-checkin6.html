How the tests on this page read. A treatment changes the price of flagged rows only and is scored out of time against keeping the blend: 2023 priced with weights fitted on 2024, and 2024 with weights fitted on 2023. "Saved" is the drop in total Tweedie deviance, at a common level per line; the keep rule is as before. Example: motor flagged rows at the GLM's price save 18,782 on A, interval 7,077 to 31,902, and 1,478 on B, so it passes.
