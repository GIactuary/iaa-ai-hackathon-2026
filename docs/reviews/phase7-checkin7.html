How the hold-out test reads. The 73,227 customers locked away on 18 October were scored once: the frozen pipeline was fitted on everyone else's 2021 to 2022 (or 2023) rows and priced the locked customers' 2023 (or 2024) rows. "Saved" is the drop in total Tweedie deviance against the W2 GLM, at a common level per line; the keep rule is as before. Example: in contents the blend saves 7,714 on A, interval 1,770 to 14,838, and 7,913 on B, so it passes.

Where I was wrong (D28) I had stated that every claim costs something, but checked it on the non-hold-out customers only. The hold-out holds one closed nil claim (0.02 incurred, 0.02 recovered), which stopped a severity fit in the refit. Nil claims are now left out of the per-peril models; that changes nothing in the frozen model, and the frozen file was rebuilt and checked identical.
